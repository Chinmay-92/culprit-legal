Culprit Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Culprit is a murder-mystery party game played on phones in the same room. We built it to collect as little as possible: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only server we run writes new cases for Culprit Premium subscribers, and it never receives your name, contacts or anything you say in a game.

Playing with nearby phones

Culprit uses your local network (Wi-Fi or Personal Hotspot) to connect directly to the other players' phones. Your chosen player name, game moves, votes and text chat messages are sent only to the phones in your game. They are not sent to us or stored after the game ends.

Microphone and voice chat

If you allow microphone access, your voice is streamed live and directly to the other phones in your game during discussion. Voice is never recorded, stored or sent to us. You can turn off microphone access at any time in iOS Settings.

New cases for subscribers (Culprit Premium)

Subscribers always have a newly written case waiting. To provide it, the app sends our case server the signed subscription record that Apple gives the app. The server checks that record with Apple's certificates and uses only its anonymous transaction number and subscription type, to keep your waiting case, count how many cases you have had this month and avoid repeating settings. That record contains no name, email, payment details or Apple Account. The case itself is written by xAI's Grok model; we send xAI only the writing instructions and the titles of cases you have already had, never anything that identifies you. Your case record is kept while your subscription is active and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We do not see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active, as described above. See Apple's privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

Culprit keeps your settings, such as your player name, sound preferences and any cases you have written, on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

Culprit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
