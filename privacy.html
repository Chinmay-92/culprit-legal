Culprit Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Culprit is a murder-mystery party game played on phones in the same room. We built it to collect as little as possible: we have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We, the developer, never receive your personal data.

Playing with nearby phones

Culprit uses your local network (Wi-Fi or Personal Hotspot) to connect directly to the other players' phones. Your chosen player name, game moves, votes and text chat messages are sent only to the phones in your game. They are not sent to us or stored after the game ends.

Microphone and voice chat

If you allow microphone access, your voice is streamed live and directly to the other phones in your game during discussion. Voice is never recorded, stored or sent to us. You can turn off microphone access at any time in iOS Settings.

AI-written cases (Culprit Premium)

If you use the case writer, Culprit sends the theme you type to Anthropic's Claude API using the Anthropic API key you enter yourself. This request goes directly from your phone to Anthropic; it does not pass through us. Anthropic's handling of that request is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy. Your API key is stored only in your device's secure Keychain and is sent only to Anthropic.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We do not see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active. See Apple's privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

Culprit keeps your settings, such as your player name, sound preferences and any cases you have written, on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

Culprit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
